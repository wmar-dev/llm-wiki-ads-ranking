## IAB Digital Advertising Format Taxonomy

According to the Interactive Advertising Bureau (IAB), the main digital advertising formats in programmatic include:

1. **Banner (Display) Ads**: Graphical ads on pages (static/animated image + text/video). Common IAB-standard sizes: 300×250 "medium rectangle," 728×90 leaderboard. Sold on CPM basis (~$2-10 CPM). Global average CTR ~0.06%.

2. **Interstitial Ads**: Full-screen ads that take over the entire screen during natural transition points in mobile apps or websites. Can be static images, HTML5 creatives, or video. 100% viewability. CTR 2-5% (5-10x higher than banners). Best suited for mobile apps.

3. **Rich Media Ads**: Advanced digital ads incorporating video, audio, 3D elements, forms, or user interactions, built with HTML5 for immersive storytelling. Generate 3x longer site visits post-click than static banners. Interaction rates can exceed 20%.

4. **Native Ads**: Ad units integrated in the user's content experience, matching the look, feel, and function of the media format. Non-intrusive, mobile-friendly. Accounted for 15-20% of programmatic budgets in 2025. CPC $0.10-$1.00; CPM $5-$15.

5. **Video Ads**: Dynamic motion-based creatives. Two main forms:
   - In-stream: pre-roll, mid-roll, post-roll inside a video player (autoplay with sound)
   - Out-stream: runs outside the website's video player (muted by default, can be skipped/scrolled)
   27x higher CTR than static banners. CPM $10-$30+; CTV can exceed $40 CPM.

6. **Push/Pop-Under Ads**: Non-traditional formats used in performance/affiliate marketing. Push ads deliver messages to device/browser (2-8% CTR). Popunders open new tab behind active window (bypass ad blockers).

## Non-Display Digital Ad Formats

7. **Search Ads (SEM)**: Text-based ads on search engines (Google PPC, Bing), triggered by queries. CPC $1-10+ (avg $5.26). CTR 3-5%; conversion 5-10%. Bottom-funnel, high-intent.

8. **Social Media Ads**: Platform-specific ads on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Pinterest, X. Blends images, video, carousel, story formats. CPM $5-15; CPC $0.50-2. CTR 1-2%; conversion 5-10%.

9. **Email Ads**: Promotional messages or sponsored content in newsletters. CPM $10-50 for sponsored placements. CTR 2-5%; open rates 20-30%. Best for retention/nurture.

## Pricing Models

| Model | Meaning | Typical Use |
|---|---|---|
| CPM | Cost per 1,000 impressions | Brand awareness, reach |
| CPC | Cost per click | Traffic, consideration |
| CPA | Cost per acquisition/conversion | Performance, bottom-funnel |
| CPV | Cost per view | Video advertising |
| CPL | Cost per lead | Lead generation |

## Key Distinction: Ad Format vs Placement

Ad format defines the creative type and tech specs (banner, video, native). Ad placement refers to the exact location or context (in-feed, pre-roll, sticky footer). The same ad format can perform 3-10x differently depending on placement.
