# Addictive Design on Online Platforms (TikTok DSA Enforcement)

Source: https://epthinktank.eu/2026/05/06/addictive-design-on-online-platforms/

**Publisher:** European Parliamentary Research Service (EPRS), European Parliament Think Tank
**Published:** May 6, 2026

## Summary

TikTok's platform employs engagement-maximizing design mechanisms — infinite scroll, autoplay, push notifications, and a highly personalised recommender system — that researchers link to problematic usage patterns. Average daily time spent on TikTok rose to **137 minutes**, up from **27 minutes in 2019**.

### Impact on minors

- Excessive social media use has been linked to poorer mental health, particularly higher levels of depression and anxiety.
- A 2025 survey found nearly half of U.S. teenagers aged 13–17 report spending excessive time on platforms, describing themselves as online "almost constantly."
- Late-night usage disrupts sleep, compounding mental health risks.
- TikTok's systems "generate systemic risks to the mental well-being of minors" through "prolonged, compulsive engagement that users struggle to control, stemming from the persuasive design choices made by the platform."
- Separately reported: TikTok is the most-used platform after midnight among children aged 13–18 in the EU, and 7% of children aged 12–15 spend 4–5 hours daily on the app.

### EU Digital Services Act (DSA) enforcement

- On **February 6, 2026**, the European Commission preliminarily found TikTok in breach of the DSA — "the first time enforcement action has not focused on illegal content, data protection or competition, but on the harmful architecture of the platform itself."
- The Commission determined TikTok "disregarded important indicators of compulsive use," including time spent at night and app-opening frequency.
- Potential remedies include "disabling key addictive features, such as infinite scroll over time" and restructuring "the platform's core architecture."
- DSA Article 35 requires Very Large Online Platforms (VLOPs) to put in place "reasonable, proportionate and effective" mitigation measures tailored to systemic risks, which may include adapting the design, features, or functioning of the service, adapting the algorithmic/recommender system, and implementing age verification and parental control tools.
- TikTok's own teen accounts include 50+ features/settings for younger users; TikTok reports removing 25M+ suspected under-13 accounts globally in the most recent quarter.

## Relevance

This is the most direct, authoritative, and current evidence on (1) how TikTok's watch-time-optimizing recommender architecture interacts with user well-being for minors, and (2) whether/how the unsupervised, real-time recommendation paradigm can be audited and regulated under the EU DSA — the Commission's preliminary finding establishes that recommender-system design itself (not just content moderation) is now a regulatory target, and that audits under DSA Article 35 focus on behavioral proxies (night-time usage, open frequency) rather than requiring algorithm transparency per se [[raw/web/tiktok-dsa-addictive-design.md]].
