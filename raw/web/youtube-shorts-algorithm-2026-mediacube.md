# YouTube Shorts Algorithm in 2026: Key Ranking Signals

**Source:** Mediacube blog
**URL:** https://mediacube.io/en-US/blog/youtube-shorts-algorithm
**Date:** accessed 2026-06-09 (describes 2026 state)

## Summary (fetched)

A practitioner-oriented breakdown of the signals YouTube's Shorts feed uses
to rank and promote videos.

## Primary Ranking Signals

- **Swipe/skip rate** — whether a viewer watches or immediately swipes away
  is treated as the core quality signal.
- **Rewatches and looping** — videos that get rewatched/looped are promoted
  further; this substitutes for "completion rate" in a feed where most
  videos are short enough to complete trivially.
- **Engagement actions** — likes, comments, shares, saves all feed into
  promotion decisions.
- **Watch history** — individual viewing patterns drive category-level
  personalization; e.g., a user who frequently watches ASMR content may see
  a feed that is "70% from such content."

## Distinction from Long-Form

Standard YouTube videos require ~10 seconds of viewing to count as a "view";
Shorts ranking instead weighs **active engagement** (replays, likes, shares)
over passive watch duration, since most Shorts are short enough that
"completion" alone is not very discriminating.

## Promotion Mechanism

New Shorts receive limited initial exposure to a small audience; based on
early engagement, the algorithm progressively expands distribution
week-over-week. Topical/niche relevance (informative vs. entertaining
content categories) also factors into promotion decisions.

## Significance

Corroborates and adds granularity to the "explore & exploit" framing from
the five-systems overview: the specific signals driving the "exploit" phase
are swipe/skip rate, rewatch/loop count, and engagement actions — with
watch-history-based category personalization driving which seed audience a
new Short is first shown to.
