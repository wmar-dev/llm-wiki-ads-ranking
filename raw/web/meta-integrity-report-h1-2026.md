# Integrity Reports, H1 2026 — Meta Transparency Center

**Source:** Meta Platforms, Inc. (Transparency Center)
**URL:** https://transparency.meta.com/reports/integrity-reports-h1-2026/
**Date:** H1 2026 (covering 2025 data)

## Summary (fetched)

Meta's H1 2026 Integrity Reports include the company's first comprehensive
disclosure of fraud and scam enforcement metrics for its ad platforms.

### Scam Ads Removed

- In 2025, Meta removed **over 159 million scam ads**.
- **92% were proactively detected** before any user reported them.
- Meta describes this as its initial comprehensive disclosure of fraud/scam
  enforcement data, and says future reports will be "more comprehensive" as
  measurement capabilities expand using advanced AI systems.

### Scam Center Accounts (Adversarial Threat Report)

- **10.9 million accounts** associated with organized "scam center" operations
  in Southeast Asia and the Middle East were removed during 2025.
- These operations were described as showing "increasing sophistication and
  industrialization."

### Nudify App Advertising

- Between November 2025 and January 2026, Meta removed **over 344,000 ads**
  promoting non-consensual intimate image ("nudify") apps across Facebook and
  Instagram.
- Meta sent cease-and-desist letters to **46 companies** violating these ad
  policies.

### Forward-Looking Statement

Meta acknowledged fraud/scam data will become more comprehensive in future
transparency reports as AI-based measurement improves.
