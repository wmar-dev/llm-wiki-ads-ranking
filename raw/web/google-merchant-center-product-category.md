## Summary (fetched)

Google Shopping Ads uses a hierarchical product taxonomy — the **Google Product
Taxonomy** — to classify every product in the feed. This taxonomy is hosted at
`www.google.com/basepages/producttype/taxonomy-with-ids.en-US.txt` and contains
~6,000+ categories organized in a multi-level tree (depth up to 7+ levels). Each
node has both a numeric ID and a breadcrumb-style full path. The taxonomy has
20+ top-level verticals; products are auto-assigned a category from product
signals (title, description, product type) but merchants can manually override
the assignment. Category assignment feeds per-category bid management in
Shopping campaigns, and the taxonomy is versioned with periodic updates.
