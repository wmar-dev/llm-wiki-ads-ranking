INTRODUCTION
This briefing paper provides Google's response to Questions 1-2 of the CMA's Request for
Information dated 14 February 2020. It focuses on search advertising on Google through
Google Ads. Display advertising is covered in a separate briefing paper.

Key topics covered:

1. THE GOOGLE SEARCH RESULTS PAGE (SERP)
2. AD RANK: AN OVERVIEW - LTV (Long-term Value) Score
3. Computing LTV - eCPM minus costs
4. Bidding: Manual vs. Automated (Smart Bidding)
5. Predicted Clickthrough Rate (pCTR)
6. Formats and Extensions
7. Predicted Creative Quality (pCQ)
8. Predicted Landing Page Experience (pLQ)
9. Thresholds and Reserve Prices
10. Actual CPC
11. Randomized General Second-Price Auction (rGSP)
12. SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO AD RANK

LTV Formula: LTV = eCPM - (impression cost + click cost)
- eCPM = bid × pCTR (expected revenue per mille impressions)
- Impression cost = (1 - pCQ) × weight
- Click cost = (1 - pLQ) × weight

Only ads with LTV > 0 are eligible to show. Ads ranked by LTV score.
Actual CPC = minimum required to clear thresholds and beat next competitor (or reserve price).
