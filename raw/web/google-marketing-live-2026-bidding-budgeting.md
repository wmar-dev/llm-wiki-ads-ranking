# Google Marketing Live 2026: Bidding and Budgeting News

**Source URL:** https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/bidding-budgeting-google-marketing-live-2026/
**Source type:** official_documentation (Google's own product blog)

## Demand-led pacing

"Demand-led pacing automatically distributes your budget across days based on
consumer search volume" — described as Google AI "better optimiz[ing] spend
to follow consumer demand — capturing more demand on peak days and reducing
spend on slower days."

## Relationship to Smart Bidding

Demand-led pacing is positioned as operating *alongside* Smart Bidding
(Target CPA/ROAS), not as a single unified mechanism: "Smarter bidding helps
you identify where your potential customers are, and dynamic budgeting
ensures you have the funds ready to capture them." Bidding finds customers;
pacing funds availability — these are described as adjacent, coordinated
systems rather than one optimization.

## Guardrails

Demand-led pacing operates within hard constraints: "never going beyond your
monthly budget and daily spending limits."

## Other 2026 bidding announcements (context)

- "Smart Bidding Exploration" widens the query universe Smart Bidding can
  target.
- "Journey-aware bidding" reads deeper/longer conversion-path signals.
- Google reports these combined updates produced an average 27% increase in
  unique converting users on Search and a 66% reduction in manual budget
  adjustments.

---

## Takeaway

No source found discloses an internal formula analogous to Meta's
"pacing multiplier x total auction value" for how Google's pacing layer
modifies the bid that reaches the Ad Rank auction. Google's public materials
describe coordination at the budget-allocation level (how much to spend per
day) rather than at the per-auction bid-value level.
