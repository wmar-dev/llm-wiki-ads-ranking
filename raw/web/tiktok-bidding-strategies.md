# Bidding Options and Strategies for Better Ad Campaigns

**Source:** TikTok For Business Blog — "Why Bidding Matters"
**URL:** https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/why-bidding-matters
**Date:** accessed 2026-06-09

## Summary (fetched)

Official TikTok for Business explainer distinguishing **bidding options**
(how the advertiser is charged) from **bidding strategies** (how TikTok's
delivery system spends the budget to hit a goal).

## Bidding Options (Pricing Models)

- **CPM (Cost Per Mille)** — charged per thousand impressions; popular with
  larger brands seeking top-funnel awareness.
- **CPV (Cost Per View)** — charged per video view or predefined interaction;
  used for video-content campaigns.
- **CPC (Cost Per Click)** — charged per click; optimizes for clicks as the
  goal, suited to driving traffic to external landing pages.
- **oCPM (Optimized Cost Per Mille)** — delivers the best price per
  conversion; particularly effective for app advertisers acquiring new users.

## Bidding Strategies (Automated Delivery)

- **Cost Cap** — a spend-based strategy that lets advertisers set a target
  CPA and "maximize results while maintaining control over their average cost
  per action," even during competitive periods. Best for efficiency-focused,
  upper-funnel objectives like reach or video views.
- **Maximum Delivery** — a spend-based strategy that maximizes conversion
  volume within a defined budget without a target CPA. CPA may fluctuate
  daily; the system optimizes purely for total conversions within budget.

## Significance

Distinguishes the **pricing model axis** (what the advertiser pays per unit
of delivery — CPM/CPC/CPV/oCPM) from the **bid strategy axis** (how
aggressively/automatically the system spends toward a goal — Cost Cap vs.
Maximum Delivery). This mirrors Google's separation of CPC bidding from Smart
Bidding strategies (Target CPA, Maximize Conversions, etc.).
