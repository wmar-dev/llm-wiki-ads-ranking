# How TikTok Recommends Content

**Source:** TikTok Support (official documentation)
**URL:** https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/exploring-videos/how-tiktok-recommends-content
**Type:** Official platform documentation
**Access Date:** 2026-06-08

---

TikTok uses recommender systems to offer a personalized experience. These systems suggest content based on your preferences, which are informed by your interactions.

## How recommender systems work

When you first sign up for TikTok, you may be invited to select categories of interest (pets, travel, etc.) to help form your For You, Explore, and LIVE feeds. If skipped, the system starts by showing recent popular content.

Initial selection looks for:
- Popular content appropriate for a broad audience
- Content influenced by your location and language settings

Once you start interacting, your interactions act as signals that help predict content you're more likely to be interested in — and what you may prefer to skip.

### Selecting and ranking content

Three main factors:
1. **User interactions** — content you like, share, comment on, watch in full or skip, accounts you follow
2. **Content information** — sounds, hashtags, number of video views, country of publication
3. **User information** — device settings, language preference, location, time zone, device type

Each recommender system selects from a large collection of eligible content and ranks based on predicted interest. Predictions also consider interactions from others with similar interests (collaborative filtering).

### Diversifying recommendations

The goal is to show relevant content while helping users discover new interests. TikTok diversifies feeds by introducing new creators and content. Content already seen is typically not re-recommended. In Following/Friends feeds, multiple posts from the same creator are not shown consecutively.

## Feed types

- **For You:** Stream of personalized content. User interactions (especially watch time) are weighted most heavily.
- **Following:** Content from creators you follow, ranked by engagement.
- **Friends:** Content from followers you follow back, plus suggested accounts.
- **LIVE:** Live videos recommended based on interactions, content info, and user info.
- **Search:** Content matched to search queries; content information (relevance to query) weighted most heavily.
- **Notifications:** Recommended content based on activity and previous notification interactions.

## User controls

Users can influence recommendations through: liking, "Not interested" feedback, refreshing feed, managing topics, dislike for comments, keyword filtering, unfollowing, and customizing search results. Personalized feeds and search can be toggled off (EU and Kazakhstan only), showing non-personalized content.
