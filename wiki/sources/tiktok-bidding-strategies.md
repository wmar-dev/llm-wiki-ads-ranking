# Bidding Options and Strategies for Better Ad Campaigns (TikTok)

**Source:** TikTok For Business Blog, "Why Bidding Matters"

**Source type:** `official_documentation` (platform documentation)

**Processing:** single-pass

---

## Summary

TikTok's official bidding guide separates **bidding options** (the pricing
model — what the advertiser is charged per unit of delivery) from **bidding
strategies** (how the delivery system spends budget toward a goal).

## Key Claims

- Bidding options: CPM, CPV, CPC, and oCPM (optimized CPM, which targets best
  price per conversion and is recommended for app-install advertisers)
  [[raw/web/tiktok-bidding-strategies.md]] *(official documentation)*.
- Bidding strategies: **Cost Cap** (target CPA, system holds average cost
  near that target) and **Maximum Delivery** (no target CPA — maximizes
  conversion volume within budget) [[raw/web/tiktok-bidding-strategies.md]]
  *(official documentation)*.
- The pricing-model vs. bid-strategy distinction mirrors Google's separation
  of CPC bidding from Smart Bidding strategies (Target CPA, Maximize
  Conversions) [[wiki/concepts/google-ad-rank-ltv-scoring.md]].
