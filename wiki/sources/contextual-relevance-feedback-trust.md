## Overview

Summarizes recent research (2024–2026) on three interconnected topics: bias amplification through AI feedback loops in recommender systems, the performance of contextual vs behavioral advertising, and the challenge of balancing ad relevance with user trust.

## Key Findings

- AI feedback loops in ranker training amplify biases over multiple retraining cycles — only 24/347 papers in a 2025 survey validated mitigations in dynamic settings. Self-play frameworks (SPRec) show promise.
- Contextual advertising matches behavioral within 5-8% on CTR while outperforming on viewability (+10-15%), brand safety, and cost (-30-40% CPM). Contextual reaches 100% of visitors vs 40-55% after consent.
- 40% of consumers find ads irrelevant. Users prefer "natural" ad experiences. The balance requires user-centric formats, AI-powered contextual targeting, and Acceptable Ads standards.
