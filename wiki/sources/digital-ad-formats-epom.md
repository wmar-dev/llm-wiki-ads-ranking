# Digital Ad Formats: IAB Taxonomy and Format Types

**Source:** Epom Blog, January 6, 2026

**Source type:** `blog_post` (ad tech vendor)

**Processing:** single-pass

---

## Summary

Comprehensive overview of digital advertising formats organized by IAB taxonomy. Covers 9 major format types across display (banner, interstitial, rich media, native, video, push/pop-under) and non-display (search, social, email) categories. Includes pricing benchmarks (CPM, CPC, CPV), performance metrics (CTR, conversion rates), and the distinction between ad format (creative type) and placement (location/context).

## Key Claims

- IAB defines 5 main display ad formats: banner, interstitial, rich media, native, and video [[raw/web/digital-ad-formats-epom.md]] *(blog post)*.
- Global average banner CTR is ~0.06%; rich media generates 3× longer site visits post-click than static banners [[raw/web/digital-ad-formats-epom.md]].
- Non-display formats include search ads (CPC $1-10+, CTR 3-5%), social media ads (CPM $5-15, CTR 1-2%), and email ads (CPM $10-50, CTR 2-5%) [[raw/web/digital-ad-formats-epom.md]].
- Ad formats and placements are orthogonal: same format can perform 3-10× differently depending on placement [[raw/web/digital-ad-formats-epom.md]].
