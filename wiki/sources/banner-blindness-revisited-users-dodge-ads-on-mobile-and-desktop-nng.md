# Banner Blindness Revisited: Users Dodge Ads on Mobile and Desktop — NN/G

## Summary

NN/G's eyetracking study confirms that banner blindness remains a robust behavioral pattern on both desktop and mobile. Key findings: the right rail received 0.8% of user attention despite occupying 25% of the content area (a 33× deficit). On mobile, inline ads are harder to avoid due to limited viewport size, but users still fail to cognitively process them. The Gestalt law of proximity means content near ads is assumed to be ad-related and also ignored. Neutral-valence ads escape the learned-avoidance filter better than emotionally charged ones.
