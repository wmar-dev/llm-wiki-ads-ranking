# YouTube Shorts Algorithm in 2026: Key Ranking Signals

**Source:** Mediacube blog, "YouTube Shorts Algorithm"

**Source type:** `blog_post` (creator-network practitioner guide)

**Processing:** single-pass

---

## Summary

A practitioner-oriented breakdown of the specific signals YouTube's Shorts
feed uses to rank and progressively promote videos, complementing the
higher-level "explore & exploit" framing from the five-systems overview.

## Key Claims

- **Swipe/skip rate** is the core quality signal for Shorts — whether a
  viewer watches or immediately swipes away
  [[raw/web/youtube-shorts-algorithm-2026-mediacube.md]] *(blog post)*.
- **Rewatches and looping** substitute for "completion rate," which is not
  very discriminating when most videos are short enough to complete
  trivially; videos that get rewatched/looped are promoted further
  [[raw/web/youtube-shorts-algorithm-2026-mediacube.md]] *(blog post)*.
- **Engagement actions** (likes, comments, shares, saves) feed into
  promotion decisions, alongside **watch-history-based personalization** —
  e.g., a user who frequently watches ASMR content may see a feed that is
  "70% from such content"
  [[raw/web/youtube-shorts-algorithm-2026-mediacube.md]] *(blog post)*.
- Standard long-form YouTube videos require ~10 seconds of viewing to count
  as a "view"; Shorts ranking instead weighs **active engagement** (replays,
  likes, shares) over passive watch duration
  [[raw/web/youtube-shorts-algorithm-2026-mediacube.md]] *(blog post)*.
- **Promotion mechanism**: new Shorts get limited initial exposure to a
  small audience, with distribution expanding progressively week-over-week
  based on early engagement; topical/niche relevance also factors into
  promotion decisions
  [[raw/web/youtube-shorts-algorithm-2026-mediacube.md]] *(blog post)*.
