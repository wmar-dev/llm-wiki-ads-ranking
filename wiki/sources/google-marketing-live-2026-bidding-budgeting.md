# Google Marketing Live 2026: Bidding and Budgeting

**Source:** Google's official "The Keyword" product blog, Google Marketing
Live 2026 announcements

**Source type:** `official_documentation`

## Summary

Announces **demand-led pacing**: Google AI redistributes an advertiser's
budget across days based on predicted consumer search demand, shifting spend
toward high-demand days and away from low-demand days, while staying within
monthly/daily budget caps. Positioned as operating *alongside* Smart Bidding
(Target CPA/ROAS) — "smarter bidding helps you identify where your potential
customers are, and dynamic budgeting ensures you have the funds ready to
capture them"
[[raw/web/google-marketing-live-2026-bidding-budgeting.md]].

## Key Claims

| Claim | Status |
|---|---|
| "Demand-led pacing" redistributes budget across days based on predicted search demand | Stated |
| Demand-led pacing is described as complementary to, not unified with, Smart Bidding — pacing controls *when/how much* budget is available; bidding controls *what* to bid | Stated |
| Demand-led pacing respects existing monthly/daily spend limits as hard guardrails | Stated |
| Combined 2026 bidding updates (incl. Smart Bidding Exploration, journey-aware bidding) reported a 27% increase in unique converting users on Search and a 66% reduction in manual budget adjustments | Stated |

## Relevance to Ads Ranking

This addresses the [[wiki/synthesis/ad-pacing.md]] /
[[wiki/synthesis/what-are-the-components-of-an-ad-ranking-system.md]] open
question for the Google side: as of the 2026 announcements, Google describes
pacing (demand-led budget redistribution) and Smart Bidding as **adjacent,
coordinated systems** rather than a single unified optimization — a contrast
with Meta's documented "pacing multiplier" approach, which folds pacing
directly into the per-auction bid-value calculation
[[wiki/sources/meta-bid-and-budget-pacing.md]].

## Open Questions

- Open question: Google's public materials do not disclose whether
  demand-led pacing (or its predecessor budget-pacing system) modifies the
  bid value submitted to the per-auction Ad Rank computation directly
  (Meta-style multiplier), or operates purely at a coarser daily-budget
  allocation level that Smart Bidding then optimizes within.
