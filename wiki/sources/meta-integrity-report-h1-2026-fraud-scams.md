# Integrity Reports, H1 2026 (Meta Transparency Center)

**Source:** Meta Platforms, Inc. (Transparency Center)

**URL:** [transparency.meta.com/reports/integrity-reports-h1-2026](https://transparency.meta.com/reports/integrity-reports-h1-2026/)

**Source type:** `official_documentation`

---

Meta's first comprehensive disclosure of fraud/scam-ad enforcement metrics.
Headline figures for 2025: **159M+ scam ads removed (92% proactive,
pre-report)**; **10.9M scam-center accounts** removed across Southeast Asia
and the Middle East (Adversarial Threat Report); **344K+ "nudify" app ads**
removed Nov 2025–Jan 2026 with cease-and-desist letters to 46 companies. Meta
frames this as a starting point, with "more comprehensive" fraud/scam metrics
promised in future reports as AI-based measurement improves.

## Dispute

This report's **159 million scam ads removed in 2025** figure is in tension
with the **134 million** figure cited in the [[wiki/sources/cfa-lawsuit-meta-scam-ads-2026.md]]
class-action complaint *(news_article)*, which also draws on Meta's internal
estimates. Both figures may be accurate under different definitions/scopes
(e.g., "scam ads" vs. a narrower "high-risk" category, or different reporting
windows) — the report does not specify methodology in enough detail to
reconcile the two. Neither figure addresses the **15 billion "higher-risk"
scam ads served daily** claim from [[wiki/sources/reuters-meta-fraud-profit-investigation.md]]
*(news_article)* and the CFA complaint, which describes ads *shown* rather
than ads *removed* — these are not directly comparable metrics (numerator vs.
denominator of the enforcement funnel).
